### 1. **Oral Reports Scheduling (6 students, 3 days, 2 reports/day)**

**Key Constraints:**
- George only on Tuesday.
- Olivia & Robert never afternoon.
- If Nina reports (not Wednesday), then *next day* Helen & Irving both report.
- Exactly 6 students report, 2 per day.

**Difficult Points:**

- **Nina’s conditional:**  
  - If Nina reports on Mon or Tue, then Helen & Irving must both report *together* the next day (Tue or Wed).  
  - Since only 2 reports per day, Helen & Irving must be the only two that day.  
  - If Nina and Helen report same day, that day must be Wednesday (no next day to trigger Helen & Irving).

- **Scheduling George, Nina, Robert on different days:**  
  - George fixed Tuesday.  
  - Nina cannot be Monday (would force Helen & Irving on Tuesday with George, exceeding 2 reports).  
  - Nina on Wednesday avoids triggering Helen & Irving condition.  
  - Robert then on Monday.

- **Morning/Afternoon restrictions:**  
  - Olivia & Robert never afternoon.  
  - George only Tuesday (morning or afternoon).  
  - Helen, Kyle, Lenore morning reports fill all morning slots → Olivia & Robert cannot report (no morning slots left).

- **If Kyle is Tuesday afternoon and Helen Wednesday afternoon:**  
  - Morning reports must fit constraints: George only Tuesday morning, Robert morning Monday, Irving morning Wednesday.  
  - Nina not reporting → no Nina condition triggered.

### 2. **Reading Club Selection (5 or 6 works from 9 total)**

**Key Constraints:**
- Max 4 French works total.
- 3–4 novels total.
- French novels ≥ Russian novels.
- If both French plays selected → Russian play not selected.

**Difficult Points:**

- **Minimum French novels:**  
  - At least 2 French novels must be selected to satisfy French novels ≥ Russian novels and total novels ≥ 3.

- **Selections that cannot happen:**  
  - No Russian novels + exactly one play → would force too many French works (violates max 4 French works).  
  - Both French plays + Russian play → forbidden.

- **Play selection dependencies:**  
  - French plays together exclude Russian play.  
  - French novels must balance Russian novels.

### 3. **Concert Composition Order (8 compositions, slots 1–8)**

**Key Constraints:**
- T immediately before F or immediately after R.
- At least two compositions between F and R.
- O is 1st or 5th.
- 8th is L or H.
- P before S.
- At least one composition between O and S.

**Difficult Points:**

- **P cannot be 7th:**  
  - Because S must come after P, and 8th is L or H, S cannot be 8th → P cannot be 7th.

- **If T is 5th and F is 6th:**  
  - R must be 1st–4th to have two compositions between F and R.  
  - O must be 1st (since 5th is T).  
  - S must be 4th or 7th to satisfy P before S and compositions between O and S.

- **If S is 4th:**  
  - O must be 1st (since O is 1st or 5th, and 5th is T).  
  - P must be before S (positions 2 or 3).  
  - T must be placed to satisfy adjacency with F or R.

### 4. **Pet Shop Daily Features (7 days, kittens & puppies)**

**Key Constraints:**
- Greyhounds on day 1.
- No breed repeats on consecutive days.
- Breed on day 1 not on day 7.
- Himalayans exactly 3 days, not day 1.
- Rottweilers not on day 7 or any Himalayan day.

**Difficult Points:**

- **If Himalayans not on day 2:**  
  - Day 2 kitten is Manx or Siamese.  
  - Siamese on day 4 possible.

- **If Himalayans not on day 7:**  
  - Some pairs of days cannot share same kitten and puppy breed (e.g., day 2 and 6).  
  - Because of Rottweiler and Himalayan restrictions, Newfoundland is the only puppy breed that can appear twice non-consecutively, but adjacency rules limit this.

### 5. **Research Team Selection (8 employees, conditions)**

**Key Constraints:**
- If Myers on team → no Ortega or Paine.
- If Schmidt on team → Paine & Thomson must be on team.
- If Wong on team → Myers & Yoder must be on team.
- Team size ≥ 4.

**Difficult Points:**

- **Schmidt and Wong cannot both be on team:**  
  - Wong requires Myers & Yoder.  
  - Schmidt requires Paine & Thomson.  
  - Myers and Paine cannot be together → conflict.

- **If Yoder not on team:**  
  - Wong cannot be on team (needs Yoder).  
  - Myers cannot be on team (needs Wong to be on team to be included).

- **If Paine not on team:**  
  - Schmidt cannot be on team (needs Paine).  
  - Myers can be on team, but Ortega cannot (Myers excludes Ortega).

- **At least one of Thomson or Yoder must be on team:**  
  - Otherwise, team size and constraints cannot be met.

### 6. **Editing Order of Articles (7 articles, 3 topics)**

**Key Constraints:**
- No two consecutive articles share topic.
- S earlier than Q only if Q is 3rd.
- S earlier than Y.
- J before G before R.

**Difficult Points:**

- **If Y is 4th:**  
  - J must be 2nd to satisfy J < G < R and no consecutive topics.

- **If G is 4th:**  
  - Y can be 6th without conflict.

- **If J is 3rd:**  
  - G cannot be 4th (would be consecutive finance).  
  - Y can be 5th.

- **H can be 2nd:**  
  - Possible to arrange without violating constraints.

### 7. **Concert Band Solo Order (6 members)**

**Key Constraints:**
- Guitarist not 4th.
- Percussionist before keyboard player.
- Keyboard player after violinist and before guitarist.
- Saxophonist after exactly one of percussionist or trumpeter.

**Difficult Points:**

- **Acceptable order example:**  
  - Violinist, trumpeter, saxophonist, percussionist, keyboard player, guitarist.

- **Keyboard player cannot be first:**  
  - Must be after violinist.

- **Guitarist before percussionist is impossible:**  
  - Percussionist must be before keyboard, keyboard before guitarist.

- **Keyboard player before saxophonist can be true.**

- **If violinist is 4th:**  
  - Trumpeter before saxophonist is not necessarily true.

**Difficult Points:**

- **Holden must be before lithographs.**

- **Farley cannot give lithographs** (since Farley before oil paintings, and oil paintings before lithographs).

- **If watercolors is 3rd:**  
  - Jiang can give lithographs 4th.

### 9. **Rugs Weaving Colors**

**Key Constraints:**
- White used only in multicolored rugs (with 2 other colors).
- Olive used only with peach.
- Forest & turquoise not together.
- Peach & turquoise not together.
- Peach & yellow not together.

**Difficult Points:**

- **If forest & peach together in a rug:**  
  - Rug is multicolored.  
  - Turquoise & yellow cannot be in same rug as peach & forest.  
  - White cannot be used (would require 2 other colors, but peach & forest already 2 colors).  
  - So white not used at all is possible.

### 11. **Shopping Center Business Locations**

**Key Constraints:**
- Pharmacy at one end; restaurant at other end.
- Restaurants separated by at least two businesses.
- Pharmacy next to optometrist or veterinarian.
- Toy store not next to veterinarian.

**Difficult Points:**

- **If shoe store at space 2:**  
  - Optometrist can be at space 5 without conflict.

- **If veterinarian at space 5:**  
  - Pharmacy must be at space 7.  
  - Restaurant at space 1.

- **If optometrist next to shoe store:**  
  - Businesses immediately adjacent to this pair must be restaurant and toy store.

- **If shoe store at space 4:**  
  - Pharmacy must be next to veterinarian.

### 12. **Sales Representatives Zones**

**Key Constraints:**
- Exactly one of Parra or Tiao in Zone 1.
- Exactly one of Tiao or Udall in Zone 2.
- Parra & Quinn together.
- Stuckey & Udall together.
- Zone 3 has more reps than Zone 2.

**Difficult Points:**

- **Valid assignments:**  
  - Parra & Quinn in Zone 3; Stuckey & Udall in Zone 2; Tiao in Zone 1.

- **Quinn cannot be in same zone as Tiao.**

- **Zone 1 can include Vegemite, Wellspring, Yardsign (concert analogy).**

### 13. **Maintenance Company Service Targets**

**Key Constraints:**
- Website target ≤ voicemail target for each client.
- Image’s voicemail < Solide’s and Truvest’s voicemail.
- Solide’s website < Truvest’s website.

**Difficult Points:**

- **If Truvest’s website < voicemail:**  
  - Solide’s website must be 1 day.

- **If Image’s website is 2 days:**  
  - Image’s voicemail must be 2 days.

- **Targets that cannot be duplicated:**  
  - 3-day website target cannot be assigned to more than one client.

### 14. **Repertory Theater Movie Schedule**

**Key Constraints:**
- Western before horror.
- Sci-fi not on screen 3.
- Romance not on screen 2.
- Horror & mystery on different screens.
- Screen 3 shows one movie at 8 P.M.
- Screens 1 & 2 show two movies each at 7 & 9 P.M.

**Difficult Points:**

- **Western & horror on same screen 2 at 7 & 9 P.M. possible.**

- **If romance before western:**  
  - Romance at 7 P.M. on screen 1.  
  - Sci-fi must be on screen 2.

- **If sci-fi & romance on same screen:**  
  - Both on screen 1.  
  - Western must begin at 7 P.M. on screen 2.

### **General Tips for Difficult Problems:**

- **Track conditional dependencies carefully:**  
  E.g., "If X then Y and Z," or "If Nina reports on Monday, then Helen & Irving must report Tuesday."

- **Use process of elimination for impossible pairs or sequences.**

- **Pay attention to exclusivity and adjacency constraints:**  
  E.g., no two same breeds on consecutive days, no two same topics consecutively.

- **When multiple conditions involve ordering, map out possible sequences to check feasibility.**

- **Remember to check total counts (e.g., number of reports, number of works selected, number of lectures per location).**

- **For "must be true" or "cannot be true" questions, focus on what is forced by the constraints, not just what is possible.**
