# Date Calculation Cheat Sheet

## 1. **Interpreting "Today", "Yesterday", "Tomorrow", etc.**
- **Yesterday** = 1 day before today.
- **Tomorrow** = 1 day after today.
- **One week ago from today** = 7 days before today.
- **One week from today** = 7 days after today.
- **10 days ago** = 10 days before today.
- **24 hours later** = 1 day after today.
- **A month ago** = Same day, previous month (watch for months with fewer days).
- **A year ago** = Same month and day, previous year (watch for leap years).

---

## 2. **Figuring Out "Today" When Given Relative Dates**
- If told "Tomorrow is MM/DD/YYYY", then **today** is one day before that date.
- If told "Yesterday was MM/DD/YYYY", then **today** is one day after that date.
- If told "The day before yesterday was MM/DD/YYYY", then **today** is two days after that date.
- If told "The deadline is MM/DD/YYYY, which is N days away from now", then **today** is N days before that date.

---

## 3. **Special Phrases and Conventions**
- **Anniversaries**: If someone started on MM/DD/YYYY and today is their N-year anniversary, then today is MM/DD/(YYYY+N).
- **Nth visit/event**: If an event happens on the same day each month, the Nth event is N-1 months after the first.
- **"Last day of [month/year]"**: Know the last day of each month (see below).
- **"First day of the year"**: Always January 1st.
- **"Last day of the first quarter"**: March 31st.

---

## 4. **Month Lengths and Leap Years**
- **Month lengths**:
  - Jan: 31, Feb: 28 (29 in leap years), Mar: 31, Apr: 30, May: 31, Jun: 30, Jul: 31, Aug: 31, Sep: 30, Oct: 31, Nov: 30, Dec: 31
- **Leap year rule**: Year is leap if divisible by 4, except years divisible by 100 unless also by 400.
  - E.g., 2000 is leap, 1900 is not, 2016 is leap, 2017 is not.

---

## 5. **Date Format Conventions**
- **MM/DD/YYYY**: Month first, then day, then year (US standard).
- **UK format**: DD/MM/YYYY. If told "today is 02/01/1987 in the UK", in MM/DD/YYYY it's 01/02/1987 (Jan 2, 1987).

---

## 6. **Counting Days Across Months**
- When adding/subtracting days across months:
  - Subtract days from current day; if result < 1, go to previous month and add days in that month.
  - When adding days, if result > days in month, subtract days in month and move to next month.

**Example**:  
- Today: 04/10/1985. 10 days ago: 04/10 - 10 = 0 → Go to March. March has 31 days, so 31 - (10 - 10) = 31. So, 03/31/1985.

---

## 7. **Handling "Nth day after/before"**
- **"N days after MM/DD/YYYY"**: Add N days, accounting for month/year changes.
- **"N days before MM/DD/YYYY"**: Subtract N days, accounting for month/year changes.

---

## 8. **Special Holidays**
- **US Thanksgiving**: 4th Thursday in November.
  - Find the first Thursday, then add 21 days.
- **Christmas Eve**: December 24th.
- **Palindrome day 2020**: 02/02/2020.

---

## 9. **Egg/Eating Problems**
- If someone buys N items and eats one per day, and "runs out today", then today is the Nth day after the purchase date (purchase date counts as day 1).
  - To find the date: Add (N-1) days to the purchase date.

---

## 10. **Tricky Phrasing**
- **"Today is the last day of [month/year]"**: Use the last day of that month/year.
- **"Today is the second day of the third month of 1966"**: 03/02/1966.
- **"Today is Christmas Eve of 1937"**: 12/24/1937.

---

## 11. **When Options Have Year Typos**
- If the correct date is not in the options, pick the closest matching date (usually the same MM/DD, even if the year is off).

---

## 12. **Step-by-Step Approach**
1. **Identify "today"** based on the problem statement.
2. **Apply the required operation** (add/subtract days, weeks, months, years).
3. **Adjust for month/year boundaries** (see month lengths and leap years).
4. **Convert to MM/DD/YYYY format**.
5. **Check options for the closest match**.

## 14. **Examples of Calculations**
- **Subtracting days**: If today is 03/05/1973, 7 days ago is 02/26/1973.
- **Adding days**: If today is 01/31/2012, 7 days later is 02/07/2012.
- **Subtracting a month**: If today is 03/05/1973, a month ago is 02/05/1973.
- **Adding a year**: If today is 01/02/1958, 50 years later is 01/02/2008.

Question: Today is Apr 10, 1985. Jane's appointment will be 3 days later. What is the date yesterday in MM/DD/YYYY?
Options:
(A) 03/10/1986
(B) 04/10/1985
(C) 04/09/1985
(D) 04/07/1985
(E) 04/11/1986
(F) 05/13/1985
Reasoning: Let's think step by step.  
We are told that "Today is Apr 10, 1985." Yesterday is one day before today, so we subtract one day from 04/10/1985, which gives us 04/09/1985. In MM/DD/YYYY format this is 04/09/1985. So the answer is (C).
Answer: (C)
