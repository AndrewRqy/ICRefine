## CHEAT SHEET: Return to Starting Point Movement Problems

### 1. **Coordinate System & Facing Direction**
- **Start:** (0, 0), facing **positive y-axis** (up/north).
- **Directions:**
  - **Forward:** +y (in current facing direction)
  - **Backward:** -y (opposite current facing direction)
  - **Right:** +x (relative to current facing direction)
  - **Left:** -x (relative to current facing direction)

#### **Turning Rules**
- **Turn Left:** 90° counterclockwise
- **Turn Right:** 90° clockwise
- **Turn Around:** 180° (reverse direction)

#### **Facing Direction Tracking**
- Use a compass:  
  - 0 = North (+y)  
  - 1 = East (+x)  
  - 2 = South (-y)  
  - 3 = West (-x)  
- **Turning:**  
  - Left: (current - 1) mod 4  
  - Right: (current + 1) mod 4  
  - Around: (current + 2) mod 4

---

### 2. **Step Tracking Table**

| Facing | Forward | Backward | Right | Left |
|--------|---------|----------|-------|------|
| North  | +y      | -y       | +x    | -x   |
| East   | +x      | -x       | -y    | +y   |
| South  | -y      | +y       | -x    | +x   |
| West   | -x      | +x       | +y    | -y   |

---

### 3. **"Always Face Forward"**
- **Ignore all turns**. Facing is always North (+y).
- **Right/Left:** Always affect x-axis.
- **Forward/Backward:** Always affect y-axis.

---

### 4. **Step-by-Step Solution Method**

1. **Initialize:**  
   - Position: (0, 0)  
   - Facing: North (unless "Always face forward")

2. **For each instruction:**  
   - **If turn:** Update facing direction.
   - **If step:**  
     - Use the table above to determine which axis and sign to update, based on current facing.
     - Add/subtract the number of steps to x or y.

3. **After all instructions:**  
   - If position is (0, 0), answer **Yes**.
   - Otherwise, answer **No**.

---

### 5. **Common Pitfalls**
- **Forgetting to update facing after each turn.**
- **Mixing up axes for right/left after turns.**
- **Not resetting facing to North for "Always face forward".**
- **Not summing steps correctly (especially with both positive and negative moves).**

---

### 6. **Quick Reference: Facing Direction Update**

- **Start:** North (0)
- **Turn Left:** (facing - 1) mod 4
- **Turn Right:** (facing + 1) mod 4
- **Turn Around:** (facing + 2) mod 4

---

### 7. **Example Walkthrough**

#### Example:  
Turn left. Take 4 steps. Turn around. Take 4 steps.

- Start: (0,0), North (0)
- Turn left: West (3)
- Take 4 steps (West): x -= 4 → (-4,0)
- Turn around: East (1)
- Take 4 steps (East): x += 4 → (0,0)
- **Back to start: Yes**

---

### 8. **Shortcuts**
- **If all steps are forward/backward and turns are always paired (turn around, then turn around again), you may end up back at start.**
- **If "Always face forward", just sum all x and y moves directly.**

---

### 9. **Sample Calculation Table**

| Step | Facing | Instruction         | Δx | Δy | New (x, y) |
|------|--------|---------------------|----|----|------------|
| 0    | North  | Start               | 0  | 0  | (0, 0)     |
| 1    | West   | Turn left           |    |    | (0, 0)     |
| 2    | West   | Take 4 steps        | -4 | 0  | (-4, 0)    |
| 3    | East   | Turn around         |    |    | (-4, 0)    |
| 4    | East   | Take 4 steps        | +4 | 0  | (0, 0)     |
