## CHEAT SHEET: 3-Object Ordering Problems

### 1. **General Approach**
- **List the objects:** Write down the three items (A, B, C).
- **Extract clues:** For each statement, write the relationship as an inequality or position.
- **Build the order:** Combine the clues to form a complete sequence.
- **Answer the question:** Map the sequence to the options.

---

### 2. **Common Relationship Types & How to Write Them**

#### **A. Left/Right/Above/Below/Newer/Older/More Expensive/Less Expensive**
- **A is to the left of B:** A _<_ B (A comes before B)
- **A is to the right of B:** A _>_ B (A comes after B)
- **A is above B:** A _<_ B (A is better than B)
- **A is below B:** A _>_ B (A is worse than B)
- **A is newer than B:** A _>_ B (A is more recent)
- **A is older than B:** A _<_ B (A is less recent)
- **A is more expensive than B:** A _>_ B (A costs more)
- **A is less expensive than B:** A _<_ B (A costs less)

#### **B. "Second from the left"/"Second-most expensive"/"Second-newest"**
- The "second" item is the **middle** one in the order.

---

### 3. **Step-by-Step Example**

#### **Example Statement:**
- "The orange book is to the left of the blue book."
- "The yellow book is to the right of the blue book."

#### **Step 1: Write inequalities**
- Orange < Blue
- Blue < Yellow

#### **Step 2: Combine**
- Orange < Blue < Yellow

#### **Step 3: Map to positions**
- Leftmost: Orange
- Middle: Blue
- Rightmost: Yellow

---

### 4. **Special Cases**

#### **A. "A is the second from the left"**
- The order is: ? A ?
- Use other clues to fill in the rest.

#### **B. "A is the most expensive"**
- A > B, A > C
- Use other clues to order B and C.

#### **C. "A is the cheapest"**
- A < B, A < C

#### **D. "A finished above B" (in a competition)**
- A < B (A did better than B; 1st is best)

#### **E. "A finished last"**
- A is in the last position (3rd place)

---

### 5. **Combining Multiple Clues**

- **Write all clues as inequalities.**
- **Chain them together.**
    - If A < B and B < C, then A < B < C.
    - If A > B and B > C, then A > B > C.
- **If a clue gives a fixed position (e.g., "A is second"), place A in the middle and use other clues to fill in left/right.**

---

### 6. **Quick Reference Table**

---

### 7. **Tips for Tricky Cases**

- **If you get stuck, write all possible orders and eliminate those that contradict any clue.**
- **If two clues seem to conflict, double-check your direction (left/right, above/below, etc.).**
- **For "second-most expensive," "second-newest," etc., always look for the middle item in your final order.**
